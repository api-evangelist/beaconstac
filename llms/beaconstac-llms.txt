## Key Concepts

- **Phygital**: Connecting physical customer touchpoints (packaging, signage, printed materials, business cards) to digital destinations using QR Codes and trackable interactions. Turns static assets into measurable data sources.
- **Smart Rules**: Uniqode-exclusive conditional routing that dynamically redirects users based on device type (iOS vs Android), time of day, location, or scan frequency — all from a single QR Code.
- **Scan Journey Intelligence**: Visualizing the full user journey after a QR scan, including time, location, device, repeat scans, and downstream behavior via GA4 integration.
- **Two-Way Contact Sharing**: After sharing your contact information via a digital business card, you can also collect the recipient's details using a built-in lead generation form.

- **Print Media**: Flyers, brochures, billboards — track offline-to-online conversions
- **Retail Displays**: Product info, digital coupons, instant mobile checkout
- **TV & Streaming Ads**: Drive instant traffic to landing pages during commercials
- **Events**: Attendee check-ins, session tracking, networking via badges and booths
- **Restaurants**: Dynamic digital menus that change by time of day (breakfast/lunch/dinner)
